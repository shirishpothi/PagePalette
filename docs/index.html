Who We Were

A Vision for Creative Sustainability

Our Mission

To build a world where every notebook is as unique as the person using it, intertwining fun with social responsibility.

Social Impact

Beyond sustainability, we donated a portion of our profits to SHINE.sg, supporting children's mental wellbeing. We paid a 5% dividend to our 75 shareholders and completed our JA journey with meaningful, measurable impact.

Our Product

The Palette Notebook

Uniquely Yours, Naturally Sustainable

Each cover was laser-cut from fully recycled HDPE plastic, featuring 35 holes for attaching customizable 3D-printed charms called "PagePals." Due to the nature of recycled materials, every cover had a unique speckled pattern—no two notebooks were ever the same.

Our Story

The Journey

Phase One

Company Formation

First meeting and introduction to Junior Achievement. Elections held and leadership team assembled with a shared vision for sustainable products.

Phase Two

Product Development

Brainstormed ideas, chose notebooks, and refined prototypes. Partnered with Semula for recycled HDPE plastic sourcing.

Phase Three

Production

Laser-cut recycled plastic covers, 3D-printed PagePals, and assembled notebooks with binding rings.

Phase Four

Christmas Fair Sales

Major sales event at school Christmas fair featuring special Christmas-themed PagePals collection.

Phase Five

Liquidation

Successfully completed JA journey. Paid 5% dividend to shareholders and donated S$225.25 to SHINE.sg.
